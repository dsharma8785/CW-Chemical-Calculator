Notes on this model

  • ClO₂ (NaClO₂ + HCl) is the preferred biocide for efficacy — cost never overrides that preference. NaOCl is dosed standalone only to cover whatever fraction of biocide duty today's ClO₂ generator availability can't reach.
  • NaClO₂/HCl flagged short-supply auto-throttles effective availability down to whatever level makes that chemical's stock last exactly until the next delivery — using as much ClO₂ as sustainably possible, not less. NaOCl/H₂SO₄ flagged short-supply needs no override, since running ClO₂ at its full mechanical availability already minimizes NaOCl use.
  • Flagged fields (amber) are placeholder assumptions pending real site/trial data.
  • H₂SO₄ = base (blowdown-driven, gated on today's CW pH — zero whenever pH is within/below the 7.8–8.0 band) + a causticity offset that scales with however much NaOCl is actually dosed today. Zero NaOCl (100% availability) → zero causticity offset.
  • ORP and CW P-alkalinity are logged for reference/trending only.
